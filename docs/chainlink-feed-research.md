# Chainlink feed types and stock stop-loss orders

Checked on 2026-11-23 at 14:25 UTC, against app revision `8d1db81`. CoW Air Rescue cannot place a stop-loss on a stock held at a broker. It could support the sale of an approved tokenized stock held on a supported chain. Chainlink already has onchain feeds for some of those tokens, but the current catalog rejects them. An oracle price can make an order eligible; CoW solvers still need liquidity to sell the exact token at or above the order's limit. [Catalog rules](../scripts/catalog.py#L59-L81) · [CoW StopLoss handler](https://github.com/cowprotocol/composable-cow/blob/c0435953ac8312a606d66c91554f2bb4d22ec686/src/types/StopLoss.sol#L21-L60) · [Air Rescue terms](../src/app/terms/page.tsx#L154-L193)

## What the app uses

The app does not load the full Chainlink directory at runtime. The [source manifest](../scripts/catalog-sources.json#L5-L43) records nine production directory URLs that also appear in [Chainlink's network registry](https://github.com/smartcontractkit/documentation/blob/main/src/features/data/chains.ts). The offline builder uses 94 retained [directory rows](../scripts/catalog-feeds.json) and 94 exact [feed approvals](../scripts/catalog-policy.json) to build the [runtime catalog](../src/lib/tokenCatalogData.json). All 94 retained rows have `docs.assetClass: Crypto` and `docs.productType: Price`. The runtime catalog has 85 approved sell token-and-chain pairs and one fixed buy token per production chain. Sepolia uses fixed test feeds and is outside this check. [Catalog builder](../scripts/catalog.py#L122-L201) · [Supported tokens](../src/lib/supportedTokens.ts#L37-L59) · [Token catalog](token-catalog.md#supported-tokens)

`docs.assetClass` identifies what Chainlink classifies; the catalog's `assetClass` describes the token's economic behavior, such as `volatile` or `usd-pegged`. The builder checks the directory class, product, visibility and lifecycle fields, proxy, `feedCategory`, and direct `USD` or `ETH` quote. The runtime only allows a `volatile` sell token and the chain's fixed buy token, with approved oracles in the same quote. It prefers common ETH, then common USD. Neither a matching symbol nor a usable proxy is an approval. [Source filter and exact approval](../scripts/catalog.py#L59-L81) · [Runtime allowlist](../src/lib/supportedTokens.ts#L94-L127) · [Oracle router](../src/lib/oracleRouter.ts#L112-L134)

## Equity references and token prices

The proxy screen finds 74 USD price rows across the literal `Equity` and `Equities` classes: 30 Ethereum, 16 Base, 14 BNB Chain, nine Arbitrum, and five Polygon. Their top-level `feedCategory` is `low` for 29, `new` for eight, `custom` for 36, and the empty string for one. This includes underlying stock and token-specific prices. The `Equity` price rows alone have `docs.marketHours: NYSE` (34) or `us_equities_24/5` (35). Chainlink distinguishes standard exchange hours from extended and overnight sessions in its [Data Feeds market hours guide](https://docs.chain.link/data-feeds/selecting-data-feeds#market-hours). Confirm the schedule for the exact proxy rather than assuming every stock feed follows it. The other 708 `Equities` rows are Arbitrum `DS` rows without a proxy. All 50 literal `TokenizedEquities` rows are Arbitrum `DS` rows without a proxy: 30 `Price` rows and 20 `Proof of Reserve` rows. Looking only for that label would miss the onchain token prices below. The token contract and oracle proxy have different jobs. [Ondo's token list](https://github.com/ondoprotocol/ondo-global-markets-token-list/blob/c2741088e2a3b323ee8c42bcbf6f010cd7476f86/tokenlist.json#L957-L961) records Ethereum TSLAon at `0xf6b1117ec07684D3958caD8BEb1b302bfD21103f`, chain ID `1`, with 18 token decimals. The 8-decimal TSLAon feed prices the token and cannot transfer it. [Ondo's product docs](https://docs.ondo.finance/ondo-stocks/overview) state that the token gives economic exposure without direct title to the underlying stock and describe eligibility and issuer onboarding limits. The [CoW RWA development listing](https://dev.swap.cow.fi/rwa/asset/TSLA) lists TSLAon separately from other Tesla-referencing tokens; that does not establish solver liquidity for a stop-loss. The [Chainlink Ondo methodology](https://docs.chain.link/data-feeds/tokenized-equity-feeds/ondo#total-return-value-calculation) multiplies the underlying share price by Ondo's `sValue` to account for dividends and corporate actions. The calculated primary price can differ from the price available to sell TSLAon, and Chainlink says the feed does not price issuer risk. [Chainlink tokenized equity risk guide](https://docs.chain.link/data-feeds/tokenized-equity-feeds#issuer-risk-is-not-priced)

The handler is asset-class agnostic: it takes two ERC-20 addresses and two Chainlink-style oracle addresses, calls `latestRoundData()` and `decimals()`, and compares the normalized sell-to-buy price ratio with the strike. A compatible TSLAon/USD proxy and the existing Ethereum USDC/USD approval could supply those two price legs. The current builder rejects `Equity` and `custom`; the app also rejects unapproved tokens and oracle routes at placement. A Data Stream needs offchain report retrieval and onchain verification, which are outside the current handler's interface. ## Recommendation

Keep the current allowlist. If stock demand justifies a pilot, review Ethereum TSLAon into the existing fixed buy token, USDC, with the exact calculated TSLAon/USD proxy. This is a candidate because the issuer and directory identify the token and its pricing unit on the same chain. It would require a narrow policy exception for the exact source, not a general `Equity` or `custom` exception. Before approval, verify the token contract and decimals, feed denomination and method, exact proxy and round validity, issuer transfer and redemption limits, and executable CoW liquidity at the intended order sizes. Test the complete order path and review product eligibility and disclosures. This check did not call the candidate contracts, request a quote, or run a settlement. [Current approval process](token-catalog.md#add-or-review-a-token) · [Ondo eligibility](https://docs.ondo.finance/ondo-stocks/eligibility) · [Air Rescue execution and oracle risks](../src/app/terms/page.tsx#L179-L207)

Define when the order may execute before launch. Chainlink's [24/5 feed guide](https://docs.chain.link/data-feeds/tokenized-equity-feeds#weekend-and-holiday-behavior) says the feed can retain its last value and stop publishing even heartbeat updates on market weekends and holidays. [Ondo's calculated feed can also freeze during a corporate action](https://docs.chain.link/data-feeds/tokenized-equity-feeds/ondo#corporate-action-handling). The app preview checks freshness for each price leg, but the order uses a single maximum age for both feeds and the handler has no market-session check. The public terms describe a 24-hour default and the app can use a lower default for a route with a lower ceiling. A last price may still pass the age check after the market closes; after it becomes stale the order cannot execute. A longer timeout allows older data and does not enforce market hours. Test the exact feed and order at closes, reopenings, holidays, halts, and corporate actions. If the product must block closed-session execution, enforce that in the execution path before enabling the route; a browser warning cannot protect an open order.
